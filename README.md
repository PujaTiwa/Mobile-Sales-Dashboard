# 📱 Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze mobile sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, payment methods, and geographical sales distribution.

## 📝 Purpose

The dashboard provides a centralized view of key sales metrics and helps identify trends across **brands, mobile models, months, payment methods, customer ratings, and cities**.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Power BI**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX**
- **Microsoft Bing Maps**
- **Excel**

## 📂 Data Source

Mobile sales transaction data containing:

- Date
- Brand & Mobile Model
- Sales & Quantity
- Transactions
- City
- Payment Method
- Customer Rating

## ✨ Features / Highlights

- 📊 KPI cards for **Total Sales, Quantity, Transactions & Average Sales**
- 📈 Monthly quantity and day-wise sales analysis
- 📱 Brand and mobile model performance
- 💳 Payment method distribution
- ⭐ Customer rating analysis
- 🗺️ City-wise sales visualization
- 🎛️ Interactive filters for **Month, Brand, Mobile Model & Payment Method**

## 💡 Business Insights & Impact

- Identifies high-performing **brands and mobile models**
- Tracks monthly and weekly sales trends
- Highlights customer payment preferences
- Provides visibility into customer rating patterns
- Helps identify strong sales markets by city
- Supports **sales monitoring, product planning, and data-driven decision-making**
